# Closed-book and hybrid lineage

The dissertation includes multiple LLM-related experiments. Their input regimes should remain distinct:

| Experiment | Relevant distinction |
|---|---|
| Closed-book annual signals | Annual model measurements without the monthly Wikipedia evidence supplied in the later pipeline |
| Monthly Wikipedia-fed signals | Monthly evidence-conditioned measurements aggregated for annual or cutoff-specific evaluation |
| Autoregressive residual hybrid | A residual correction using a particular annual aggregation of the monthly panel |

The residual hybrid is not a direct test of the earlier closed-book annual signals. Its sample and persistence comparison also differ from the full-panel established benchmarks.

When following a result, identify the generating routine, source panel, temporal window and averaging convention. Historical summaries that were not supported by the retained artefacts remain unresolved or withdrawn. A public code export does not reinstate them.
