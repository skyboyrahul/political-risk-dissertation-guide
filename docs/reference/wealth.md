# Wealth comparison

The wealth analysis asks whether model error varies with country income classification. Its interpretation depends on the exact sample, signal regime, classification vintage and error definition.

Keep the full annual closed-book country sample distinct from the smaller monthly panel and the matched-model comparison. A monotonic ordering, a statistical test of association and a difference between measurement regimes are different claims. A result on one sample does not automatically establish the same claim on another.

The selected annual producer source is available under [research-code](https://github.com/skyboyrahul/political-risk-dissertation-guide/tree/main/research-code). Licensed observations, archived responses and empirical error tables are excluded. The source inventory documents which helpers and external inputs the producers require.

Older summaries with unresolved lineage remain qualified. Use the dissertation's current text and its evidence record rather than reconstructing a claim from a nearby estimate.
