# Wikipedia provenance

Wikipedia is the evidence source for the monthly measurements. Reconstructing a historical measurement requires more than knowing the present-day article URL: it requires the relevant content, revision and retrieval context, the selection rule, the prompt and the model configuration.

The retained legacy material does not establish the exact historical revision or retrieval date for every measurement. A current extraction can demonstrate what the parser does now, but it cannot fill that historical gap retrospectively.

The research used Portal material first and Year-in-Country material for missing cells in the retained panel. Combining both sources in a new run would create a different evidence regime.

The public release explains and exposes selected assembly code. It excludes bulk Wikipedia caches, rendered research prompts, the licensed scoring guide and real PRS anchors. Any later redistribution of source excerpts needs appropriate attribution and compliance with the source's terms.

Read [the monthly pipeline](monthly-pipeline.md) and the supplementary source inventory in [the guide repository](https://github.com/skyboyrahul/political-risk-dissertation-guide/tree/main/research-code).
