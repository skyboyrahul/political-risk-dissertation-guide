# Rater disagreement and model origin

The rater analyses examine disagreement and home-country contrasts when models assess shared evidence. They address the stability and validity of measurement.

GDI summarises spread between raters. It does not directly measure accuracy, event severity or conflict intensity. A home-country contrast depends on the model set, comparison group, sample construction and inference design. Model version changes are also relevant.

Inspect the selected [origin-analysis source](https://github.com/skyboyrahul/political-risk-dissertation-guide/tree/main/research-code), including fixed effects, paired comparisons, finite-sample inference and sensitivity routines. The required archived signals and empirical output tables are not included in this public release.

The observed contrasts do not identify a general causal effect of a model's national origin. Missingness and available-case selection must be read alongside the reported comparisons. Public source inspection and a full empirical re-estimation are separate verification steps.
