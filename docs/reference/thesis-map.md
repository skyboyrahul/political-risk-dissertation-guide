# Reading beside the dissertation

Use this guide as a companion to the submitted dissertation. The full dissertation and its private source archive are not redistributed in this release.

- **Chapters 1 and 2:** establish the practical problem, the measurement question and the relationship to prior work.
- **Chapter 3:** defines the target, data, established benchmarks, LLM measurements and temporal evaluation. Follow [the methods route](../methods.md).
- **Chapter 4:** reports empirical comparisons, uncertainty and validity checks. Use [the result route](../results.md) to identify public source and restricted evidence requirements.
- **Chapter 5:** interprets the findings and examines bounded uses and limitations. Follow [interpretation](../interpretation.md).
- **Appendices:** carry detailed protocols and robustness records; align their version with the dissertation under review.

This public guide preserves the distinction between exact evidence, derived reconstructions, external inputs and unresolved lineage. Those labels describe different evidential states, not different levels of confidence in a synthetic demonstration.
