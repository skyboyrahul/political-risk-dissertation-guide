# Chapter 3: methods

Read this route alongside Chapter 3. It follows the analytical decisions in the study, from the target and established benchmarks to LLM measurements and temporal evaluation.

1. Establish the annual target and distinguish its level from its year-to-year change. The annual series was the affordable ground-truth input for this project; ICRG also publishes monthly ratings.
2. Examine the [established benchmarks](reference/established.md), their feature representations and persistence comparison.
3. Check [forecast timing](reference/strict-lag.md). A same-year feature is appropriate for a nowcast with a stated cutoff; it cannot establish an ex-ante forecast.
4. Follow the [monthly pipeline](reference/monthly-pipeline.md), from evidence assembly and prompt contracts to monthly signals and annual features.
5. Read the [Wikipedia provenance limitations](reference/wikipedia.md) before treating historical measurements as exactly reconstructible.
6. Separate the [closed-book and hybrid experiments](reference/hybrid-lineage.md) from the monthly Wikipedia-fed panel.

The public repositories preserve selected research implementations and document any export adaptations. The default demonstrations use synthetic data. Their purpose is to make the methods inspectable and executable without exposing the study's licensed observations.
